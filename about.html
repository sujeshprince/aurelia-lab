Our standard

We publish the number, not the story.

Most skincare marketing is built on the presence of an ingredient rather than its dose. A formula can list niacinamide and still deliver almost none of it. We started Aurelia Lab in 2016 to remove that ambiguity: every active on every carton is listed with its exact percentage, and every claim is backed by an instrumental or clinical measurement.

If a concentration is below the threshold where published research shows an effect, we leave the ingredient out entirely rather than use it as decoration on the label.

How we formulate

Four rules we don’t bend

I.

Efficacy before elegance

If an active works at 5%, we use 5% — even when that means a formula is slightly less cosmetically perfect. Texture matters, but it does not outrank results.

II.

Nothing that only smells good

Fragrance and essential oils are the most common cause of contact sensitisation in skincare. We omit both, in every product, without exception.

III.

Barrier first, always

Every acid and retinoid we sell is buffered with ceramides, panthenol or squalane, so the actives can do their work without compromising the skin’s defence system.

IV.

Human evidence

We run instrumental testing on at least 30 volunteers per formula, including people with eczema and rosacea, before anything reaches the shelf.

V.

Refillable by default

Heavy glass outer bottles stay on your shelf. Lightweight refill pods ship in paper. Returned empties earn 15% off the next order.

VI.

No animal testing, ever

Leaping Bunny certified since launch. We do not sell in markets that require animal testing, and we do not use suppliers who do.

History

Ten years, sixteen formulas

We release roughly one new product a year. Most of the work is reformulating what already exists.

Founded in Copenhagen

Two formulation chemists leave a contract manufacturer to build a range with published concentrations.

2018

The barrier range

Ceramide Barrier Moisturizer launches after eighteen months of stability work. It remains our best-selling product.

2020

Fragrance-free commitment

We reformulate the entire catalogue to remove fragrance and essential oils, and publish the reason why.

2022

Refill programme

Returnable glass and paper refill pods roll out across all sixteen products.

2024

Encapsulated retinol

A time-release 0.3% retinol system launches with a 12-week tolerance study across 84 participants.

2026

Sixteen, and holding
