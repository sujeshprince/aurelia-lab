The actives

Four molecules doing the real work

We publish every concentration on the carton, because “contains niacinamide” means nothing without a number.

5%

Niacinamide

Regulates sebum, tightens the look of pores and interrupts pigment transfer. We cap at 5% — the point where benefit plateaus and irritation begins.

0.3%

Encapsulated Retinol

Time-released so it converts to retinoic acid slowly through the night, stimulating collagen without the peeling that sinks most retinol routines.

2%

Hyaluronic Acid

Three molecular weights hydrate the surface, the mid-epidermis and the deeper layers at once. Applied to damp skin, it holds water where it belongs.

15%

L-Ascorbic Acid

The only form of vitamin C with robust evidence behind it. Stabilised with ferulic acid and vitamin E so it stays potent to the last drop.
