Yes, but not in the same layer. Use vitamin C in the morning and retinol at night. Both are pH-dependent and work at different pH ranges, so layering them directly can inactivate the vitamin C and increase irritation. Separating them by twelve hours gives you the full benefit of each.

Hydration and barrier comfort change within days. Pore appearance and oiliness typically shift between weeks two and four. Pigmentation and fine lines need a full twelve weeks, because you are waiting on cell turnover cycles rather than on the product itself. Take a photo in consistent lighting at the start — perception is unreliable over that timeframe.

Avoid the Retinol 0.3% Night Serum and the BHA 2% Clarifying Solution. Everything else in the range is formulated without retinoids, and our azelaic, niacinamide and hyaluronic products are commonly used during pregnancy. This is general information, not medical advice — check with your doctor or midwife.
